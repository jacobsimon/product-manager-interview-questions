## About this guide

Product manager interviews test a wide surface: product sense, execution, estimation, strategy, behavioral storytelling, and often light technical fluency. This primer maps each question type to a framework and a practice set.

## The interview loop

1. Recruiter screen
2. Hiring manager screen: behavioral + background
3. Phone screen: product sense, estimation, execution
4. Take-home or case presentation (company-dependent)
5. Onsite: product sense, execution, strategy, behavioral, technical, leadership

## Question types and frameworks

### Product sense / design

"Design a product for X." "Improve product Y."

Approach: clarify the goal, segment users, pick one segment and their sharpest pain point, brainstorm wide, prioritize with explicit criteria, define success metrics. Spend more time on the problem than the feature list.

### Execution / analytical

"Metric X dropped 15% overnight. What do you do?" "What's the north-star metric for product Y?"

Approach: internal vs external causes, segment the drop (platform, geography, user cohort), correlate with launches and seasonality, state your hypothesis and how you'd confirm it.

### Estimation

"How many X in Y?"

Approach: top-down from population or bottom-up from supply. State assumptions out loud, sanity-check the final number, round aggressively.

### Strategy

"Should company X enter market Y?"

Approach: market attractiveness, company's right to win, competitive response, risks, and a recommendation. Interviewers grade the recommendation, so make one.

### Behavioral

"Tell me about a time..."

Approach: STAR with emphasis on your decisions and quantified results. Prepare 6-8 stories covering conflict, failure, influence without authority, prioritization, and shipping under constraints.

## Practice questions

| Type | Question |
|------|----------|
| Product sense | Design a fridge for the visually impaired |
| Product sense | Improve Google Maps for commuters |

| Strategy | Should Spotify launch a hardware product? |
| Behavioral | Tell me about a product decision you got wrong |
